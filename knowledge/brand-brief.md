## audience

segments:
1. **Gift buyers — HIGHEST VALUE** [stated]. Buying trigger: a birthday,
   an anniversary, a milestone for someone they know well. Main objection:
   will it arrive in time, and will it actually look good. Not a fan of the
   fandom themselves — they are buying for someone who is.
2. **Fans / self-purchasers.** Buying trigger: identity and self-expression.
   Main objection: price versus a mass-market sneaker.
3. **Businesses, teams, event organisers.** Buying trigger: branded kit for
   a group. Main objection: minimums, timeline, consistency across units.

- highest_value_segment: **gift buyers** [stated]
- Consequence for content: the primary reader is usually NOT the person who
  will wear the item. Write to the giver. The emotional payload is the
  reaction of the person receiving it, not the specs of the shoe.

## visual_identity

- palette: **segment-conditional** [stated]
  - **black + red** — private customers (gift buyers, fans, B2C)
  - **black + gold** — business, teams, events (B2B)
  - gold also appears in the logo [observed]
  - Rule: the audience decides the palette. A carousel for gift buyers is
    black + red throughout. A set never mixes the two variants. If an agent
    cannot tell which audience a post addresses, it has not finished step 1.
- typefaces: large bold Hebrew headings, with Japanese/Chinese characters
  used as graphic elements [observed]
- typefaces, PROPOSED — awaiting manager approval:
  - headline: **Rubik** at 800/900. Geometric, modern, very strong in
    Hebrew at heavy weights, reads as streetwear rather than corporate.
  - body: **Assistant** at 400/600. Clean, highly legible in Hebrew at
    small sizes, pairs quietly with Rubik.
  - alternative if Rubik feels too soft: **Heebo** at 800/900 — more
    neutral, slightly colder.
  - Both are free and must be bundled in the repo as font files, not
    loaded from a CDN at render time.
- photography_style: real finished customer products, shot in the studio;
  authentic and work-focused rather than catalogue-sterile [observed]
- mascot: **Shin** — a white cat in a red beret holding a custom shoe,
  with several character variants [stated]
- production_constraints:
  - **no Hebrew text inside AI-generated video.** Hebrew is added in post
    [stated]
  - the same applies to AI-generated images: Hebrew is rendered from a
    template, never drawn by a model

- peaks: Israeli holiday calendar; birthdays run year-round and are the

  occasion is selling something that cannot arrive.
